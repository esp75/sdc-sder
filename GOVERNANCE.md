# Governance (v0)

This file sets out how the programme makes decisions until the SDC Institute has a legal entity and an independent review group. They implement principles 6 and 7 of [Vision and Principles v0.1](spec/vision-and-principles-v0.1.md).

## How a method or rule changes

1. **Proposal.** Anyone can propose a change by opening an issue or a pull request. A proposal must give its rationale, its sources and its expected effect on outputs.
2. **Comment period.** Substantive changes stay open for comment for at least 30 days. Substantive changes are those that affect methodology weights or tables, Rulebook rules, or principles. Safety-critical corrections may be fast-tracked; the reason must be stated.
3. **Decision with reasons.** Each decision records whether the proposal was accepted, rejected or deferred, and why. It goes in the pull request and in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
4. **Versioning.** Every assessment and claim records the method or Rulebook version it used. A later change never alters the meaning of an earlier result.

## Corrections

Errors are corrected openly. The corrected document gets a dated *Correction* note that says what changed. If a paper's central claim no longer holds, it is marked *Withdrawn*, not deleted.

## Conflicts and recusal

- Every contributor to a method decision declares their relevant interests in the pull request.
- A person or organisation involved in a claim or an assessment does not take part in reviewing it or in any appeal about it.
- The originator's interests are declared in [DISCLOSURES.md](DISCLOSURES.md).

## Next step

These interim rules end when an independent expert group is constituted, with published membership, terms of reference and conflict declarations. [papers/ROADMAP.md](papers/ROADMAP.md) tracks progress.
