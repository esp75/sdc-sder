# Names, marks and conformity claims

The copyright licenses in this repository cover text and code. They do **not** grant rights to the names "Space Debris Credit", "SDC", "Space Debris Exposure Rating" or "SDER", or to any associated logo, when those are used in a way that suggests official status, endorsement or certification.

## Allowed without asking

You may say what your work does and where it comes from:

- "implements the public SDC/SDER principles v0.1"
- "uses the open-source `sder` reference scoring code"
- "based on SDC and SDER, originated by Maxime Kadri"

## Not allowed

Do not imply any of the following, because none of them exist yet:

- an official, approved or certified SDER rating
- an issued, verified or retired SDC
- endorsement by the originator, the SDC Institute or any affiliated organisation

In particular, do not label results "SDER assessed", "SDER rated", "SDC verified", "SDC issued" or "certified" until a published, independent assessment process exists.

The output of the code in this repository is an **illustrative research score**. Label it that way wherever it is shown.
