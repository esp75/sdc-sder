Who is responsible for the risk left in orbit?

We propose a way to describe a satellite mission's debris exposure and, separately, to verify actions that reduce risk. The principles, papers, data and code are open for consultation and peer review.

Current status: SDC and SDER are proposals. There is no official rating, issued credit, certification or tradable market. The principles, research papers and reference code are openly licensed for consultation.

The idea in three steps

  1. Describe the mission and its lifecycle.
  2. Review exposure, evidence and uncertainty.
  3. Verify any claimed improvement separately.

Carbon-credit systems offer an analogy for independent verification and avoiding double counting. Orbital risk is different from carbon and has no simple tonne-equivalent.

Research

Papers and reference code

  • Space Debris Credits: A Market-Based Framework for Governance and Environmental Stewardship in Orbit, Kadri & Shishkina, Strauss Center Space Traffic Conference 2026
  • Working paper series: legal pathways, the SDER and ΔR methodology, claim integrity, and a ranking of 18,036 objects in low Earth orbit from open catalogue data. Every paper is open for outside peer review.
  • Rulebook, consultation draft v0.2: the rating, the credit, verification, governance, the Equitable Space Access Fund and phased adoption. Also as a PDF and an interactive version.
  • Open-source reference implementation (Python, Apache-2.0)
